QDS Foundations & Theoretical Explainability

Why Can't Eve Clone the Signature?

By the Quantum No-Cloning Theorem (Wootters & Zurek 1982), non-orthogonal states $|0 angle, |1 angle, |+ angle, |- angle, |+i angle, |-i angle$ cannot be cloned. Any attempt by Eve to measure and duplicate the states collapses them into Eve's chosen basis, introducing an irreducible ~33.3% error rate upon Bob's verification!

Why Dual Thresholds (sv, sa)?

Physical optical fibers and detector dark counts cause baseline noise (p0 ~ 3%). The verification threshold sv=10% allows normal noise through. The abort threshold sa=20% guarantees that active forgeries (~33%) are reliably rejected with exponentially small False Acceptance Rate bounded by Hoeffding's inequality.
